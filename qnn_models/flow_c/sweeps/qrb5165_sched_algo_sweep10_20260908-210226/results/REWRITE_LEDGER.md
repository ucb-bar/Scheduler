## 3. Granularity: the cut decision per network and rung

Two distinct reasons, both measured:

* on `dronet_s*` and `mlp_control_s*` the added dispatch alone exceeds the whole network's critical path, so no assignment of the pieces can win;
* on `yolov8_nano_s*` and `fastdepth` the dispatch is affordable, but Phase 1R's rewrites already made every lane reachable whole, so a cut buys no placement it does not already have — and placement is the only thing the slicing study found cutting ever buys.

`vint` is the exception and keeps its shipped two-tile split: there the cut genuinely does buy placement, because the monolith runs nowhere but the CPU while the encoder tile composes on the DSP.
